# Feedback about AI Producer

When `send_feedback` is among your tools, it carries what the user thinks of AI Producer itself to the team that builds it: something that broke, a suggestion, something they wish it could do, or how a result or the experience felt. A change they want in their own video is an edit request, not feedback.

- The user asks you to pass something on, report a problem, or tell the team: send it with `source` `user`, without asking again.
- The user voices such a reaction without asking - frustration with a failure, praise, a wish - offer once, in one sentence that states the gist, to send it to the AI Producer team. Send it only after they agree, with `source` `agent`. Do not offer again for the same point, and never send anything the user did not say or agree to send.
- AI Producer refuses what the user asked for because it cannot do it yet: a video longer than it accepts (`source_too_long`) or a file larger than the upload limit, or a frame shape it cannot reframe to (`source_aspect_conflict`). A video with no speech or no audio track starts a project, as [A video with no speech](../SKILL.md#a-video-with-no-speech) says; where a server still refuses one (`source_no_speech`, `source_no_audio`), it is this kind of refusal too. In the message that explains the refusal and what they can do now, offer once, in one sentence, to tell the team they wanted it, because a need the team hears about can become something AI Producer does. If they agree, send it as a `request` with `source` `agent`, saying what they wanted to make and the limit it met, for example that they wanted to edit a two-hour talk and the longest video it accepts is 30 minutes, with `project_id` when a project exists. Do not offer for a refusal about their account or usage (credits, a daily limit, signing in), a damaged or wrongly uploaded file, or one a retry may clear.
- The user asks for something AI Producer has no way to do: no tool you have and nothing you can build in the composition covers it, such as a dubbed voice in another language or a project file for another editing program. Say plainly that it cannot be done here, offer what comes closest if anything does, and in the same message offer once to tell the team they wanted it. If they agree, send it as a `request` with `source` `agent`, saying what they wanted to make and why. Offer only for a gap you are sure of after checking your tools and this skill, never for something you have not tried.
- The user asks for the same change a third time, because your first two attempts at it still did not give them what they wanted. Before the third attempt, offer once to tell the team what keeps going wrong, then make the attempt either way. If they agree, send it with `source` `agent` and `sentiment` `negative`, as a `bug` when the result is broken and an `experience` otherwise, naming what they asked for, what came out each time, and the project. A user who refines what they want is not repeating a change; this is for the same request coming back unmet.
- Put the steps or context the user gave in `message`, and never paste the conversation, a transcript or file contents.
- Then tell the user in one sentence that it reached the team.
