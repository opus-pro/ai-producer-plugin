# v1.2.5

## Changes

### Changed

- Seat the speaker in an inset card on a portrait canvas: every portrait seat keeps side margins, the low-centered card at about 78% of the width is the default, the landscape video default places the speaker in that card below the asset, a head that does not fit a width-bound seat may be answered by narrowing it as well as deepening it, and the PIP example takes the near-square card as example values. ([#70](https://github.com/opus-pro/ai-producer-plugin/pull/70))
- Simplify the editorial defaults: open with a presenter punch-in and whoosh, a black canvas with near-black surfaces, purposeful visual animation, and no background music; authored copy is limited to core values, units, and disambiguating labels by the new on-screen text rules, which the framing skill defers to. ([#69](https://github.com/opus-pro/ai-producer-plugin/pull/69))
