# v1.2.12

## Changes

### Added

- Send product feedback to the AI Producer team when the user asks or agrees to share it, while leaving personal details out of the message. ([#129](https://github.com/opus-pro/ai-producer-plugin/pull/129))
- Add conditional editing guidance for videos the service identifies as having no speech, using the footage and the user's words to plan visual moments. ([#131](https://github.com/opus-pro/ai-producer-plugin/pull/131))

### Changed

- Place pre-made sound effects from the library, matching each sound's timing to the motion or moment it accompanies. ([#129](https://github.com/opus-pro/ai-producer-plugin/pull/129))
- Keep the CDN-hosted decision card and caption-preview videos outside the installed plugin package. Existing card pins continue to load the same files. ([#130](https://github.com/opus-pro/ai-producer-plugin/pull/130))
- Read a Motion Library design for the project's known orientation instead of fetching every supported orientation. ([#131](https://github.com/opus-pro/ai-producer-plugin/pull/131))

## Compatibility

- Editing videos without speech depends on service availability; the conditional guidance does not enable that capability by itself. ([#131](https://github.com/opus-pro/ai-producer-plugin/pull/131))
