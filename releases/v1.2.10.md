# v1.2.10

## Changes

### Added

- Name each effect the agent writes with a short `data-title`, so the editor timeline labels it with that name instead of its composition id. ([#111](https://github.com/opus-pro/ai-producer-plugin/pull/111))

### Changed

- Design the fine cut instead of asking for it: a new project asks two rounds, rough cut and finishes, while the user's own words still take precedence. The default direction leads with frequent real B-roll, 3D for abstract ideas, a palette taken from the footage and a new visual beat every 4 to 6 seconds, and B-roll research can fall back to free-licensed media libraries with source, author and license recorded. ([#108](https://github.com/opus-pro/ai-producer-plugin/pull/108))
- Fill the Claude directory listing: the display name `AI Producer by OpusClip`, a user-facing description, and the privacy policy, terms of service and support links. ([#107](https://github.com/opus-pro/ai-producer-plugin/pull/107), [#109](https://github.com/opus-pro/ai-producer-plugin/pull/109))

## Compatibility

- The service may still list a fine-cut question as pending; this version ignores it. Older installs keep asking it until they update. Existing plugin and marketplace identifiers remain unchanged.
